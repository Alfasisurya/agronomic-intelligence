Historical climate — ERA5 reanalysis
Copernicus Climate Change Service via Open-Meteo
Monthly rainfall, temperature, and humidity normals for 1991–2020 are computed in the browser from the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API (ERA5 and ERA5-Land, CC BY 4.0).

ECMWF SEAS5 — Seasonal climate forecast
ECMWF via Open-Meteo

https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/seasonal-forecast-api
